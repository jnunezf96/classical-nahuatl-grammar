## Batch 1, Group 1: Integrated supplements and short pronominal NNCs

- Sections: §18.1, §18.2
- Atoms: 27
- Proposed direction: 11 both; 16 reading only
- Writing job: Validate the preposed subject-or-object supplement and antecessive VNC before offering integrated ō placement, enforce the short-pronominal utterance boundary, and preserve licensed cāti spelling variants without confusing graphically identical constructions.
- Reading job: Show why the antecessive morph belongs logically to the VNC even when written on the supplement, why the integration has no distinct English translation, and why neh or teh is a complete NNC but not an independent utterance.
- User/application split: The user chooses integrated or ordinary antecessive placement only when both are licensed and supplies the intended larger composition for a short pronominal NNC; the application enforces placement conditions and utterance completeness.
- Control policy: Reuse composition order and a conditional antecessive-placement choice; do not add an integration toggle when the structural prerequisites are absent, and present nonsemantic spelling variants as cues.
- Proposal: Place antecessive ō on a preposed supplement only in the licensed integrated structure, and keep short personal-pronominal NNCs inside a larger composition unless they are principals adjoining another constituent.
- Status: ACCEPTED
- Exactly observed: 27/27
