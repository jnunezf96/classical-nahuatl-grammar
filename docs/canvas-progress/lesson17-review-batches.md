## Batch 1, Group 1: Supplementation foundation

- Sections: §17.1, §17.2
- Atoms: 44
- Proposed direction: 10 both; 34 reading only

- Proposal: Build supplementation from two complete nuclear clauses while preserving the affixal personal pronoun as head and the added clause as supplement.
