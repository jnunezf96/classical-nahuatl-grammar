# Lesson 21 atom-job review batches

All 161 atoms are included exactly once. Unaccepted groups remain proposals; implementation credit appears only after accepted jobs pass their exact checks.

## Batch 1, Group 1: Passive foundation and valid active Sources

- Sections: §21.1
- Atoms: 19
- Proposed direction: 12 both; 7 reading only
- Writing job: Validate the active Source, reject intransitive and nonspecific-only Sources, delete the active subject, promote the selected specific object from objective to nominative, and replace the active stem with its nonactive Result.
- Reading job: Show the active-to-passive transformation, the promoted participant, the deleted source subject, and why a named agent requires an active Nahuatl clause.
- User/application split: The user supplies the intended active Source and requests passive voice; the application determines eligibility and performs deletion, promotion, case change, and nonactive-stem selection. No agent-retention choice is valid.
- Control policy: Reuse Source, object, and voice controls; do not ask the user to choose case, delete the source subject, retain an agent, or substitute impersonal voice.
- Proposal: Build a passive Result only from an active VNC with a specific object, promote that object, remove the source subject completely, and use the licensed nonactive stem.
- Status: ACCEPTED
- Exactly observed: 19/19

## Batch 1, Group 2: Passive formula and single-object promotion

- Sections: §21.2, §21.2.1
- Atoms: 26
- Proposed direction: 14 both; 12 reading only
- Writing job: Promote the sole specific projective object to subject, preserve its person and number, discard every trace of the source subject, and recognize a typed silent third-person object as a real promotable object.
- Reading job: Show the source object and passive subject side by side, explain why different active subjects collapse to the same passive, and make silent-object presence visible without inventing a surface morph.
- User/application split: The user chooses the intended active object when composing the Source; once passive is requested, the sole eligible object promotes automatically. A silent versus sounded third-person realization is derived from the Source, not chosen again.
- Control policy: Reuse object-person, number, and voice controls plus clickable formula positions; do not add promotion, nominative-case, deleted-agent, or silent-object-presence switches.
- Proposal: For one specific projective object, move its person and number into the passive subject positions, including a grammatically present silent third-person object, and generate the ordinary intransitive passive formula.
- Status: ACCEPTED
- Exactly observed: 26/26
