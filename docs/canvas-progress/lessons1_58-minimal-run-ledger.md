- Run only the subjects and contrasts that the lesson actually names.
- Cross two dimensions only when the lesson states that they interact.
- Check the exact typed Source, grammatical operation, and Result—not merely route acceptance.

- Expose only genuine choices. Derived class, shape, agreement, order, and realization facts remain automatic unless Canvas presents a real alternative.
- Verify the owner, contradiction check, visible cue, and browser cache path before calling the lesson complete.

## Lessons 1–58 two-pin rotation and rhyme map

This map is a compression plan, not merely a list of connections. It aligns lessons by grammatical movement, not by matching words or lesson numbers. **Emptiness** is the lesson's unresolved typed structure or genuine choice. **Fullness** is its owner-issued Result or complete reading after the required choices and automatic consequences have settled. Pinning emptiness and fullness to the same reusable seam turns each lesson into a loop; rotating the loops reveals which arcs can occupy the same canonical machinery.

The two pins are joined only at a licensed handoff. A full Result may become a later lesson's empty Source only when the consumer accepts that exact owner-issued Result. Similar spelling, translation, example identity, or a copied formula cannot join the pins.

Lessons 40–58 are dimensionally pre-coordinated here from their authoritative Canvas atom denominators and named run topics. This does not mark those lessons accepted or implemented before their lesson-by-lesson review. Rotation predicts where their accepted rules should meet existing machinery; only the canonical owner and its exact checks may execute that meeting.

Rotation signatures are analogies, not new grammar rules:

- `F` — formation: typed Source through Grammar to Result.
- `C` — continuation: an exact Result enters another owner as typed Source.
- `R` — reading: a complete expression is turned toward its typed analysis.
- `P` — composition: complete units enter an unresolved relation and leave as one larger Result.
- `→` shows the dominant direction; `↔` shows that writing and reading must both remain available; `/` shows two licensed outgoing orientations.

Lessons rhyme when they share one or more final-column axes. The axes organize checks; they never authorize a route.

When two rotated arcs overlap, the engine should store the operation once and let both lessons use it. Keep separate machinery only when Canvas changes what may fill the vacancy, which facts survive, which relation is selected, or how the Result is realized. Apparent recursion is normally a complete typed unit re-entering the same filling operation; it is not permission to build a lesson-specific recursive engine. Consequently, compatible grammatical properties from several lessons may coexist on one exact Source and Result.

### Executed all-route inside-out cast

Every canonical application route now receives one self-updating compatibility signature derived from its existing typed operation declaration. The signature records six fields: **requires present**, **requires absent**, **adds**, **removes**, **preserves**, and **emits**. It does not read lesson numbers, example identities, expected formulas, or expected surface strings.

The cast currently covers all **100 canonical routes** and all **441 accepted route-axis jobs**. It collapses their repeated handoff machinery into six typed boundary seams: phonological unit, particle Result, VNC Result, NNC Result, clause Result, and general typed structure. A seam only carries an exact Result to the next owner. It never declares that every producer may enter every consumer; the consumer owner must still authorize the exact Result and all of its grammatical requirements.

This gives the common inside-out procedure for Lessons 1–58:

`requires present/absent → apply local change → preserve exact history → emit typed Result → next owner validates the same Result`

Two lesson arcs may be superimposed when the inner arc emits the unit kind required by the outer arc, the inner additions do not conflict with anything the outer arc requires absent, and the outer owner accepts the exact Result. They may share the boundary seam and movement coordinate while keeping different local operations and realizations. This is why the general cast independently rediscovers the Lesson 27 VNC-result seam into the Lessons 37–39 deverbal-NNC owner, and also discovers the inverse NNC-result seam used by licensed denominal VNC formation.

The automatically listed pairings are topology candidates until the receiving owner authorizes a real Result. A blocked owner Result is a grammatical boundary, not a missing connection. This prevents the rhyme map from manufacturing combinations merely because two operations mention the same broad unit kind.
